📲 Installing APKs on Android (Quick Guide)

Follow these 3 simple steps to install our verified standalone application packages on any Android phone:

1
Download APK Tap "Download APK" or scan the QR code with your phone camera to download the installer file.
2
Allow Unknown Apps When Android prompts "File might be harmful", tap Download anyway, then tap Settings → Allow from this source.
3
Install & Run Tap Install. For bridge apps (like Udan), grant notification and SMS access when prompted.

Scan with Smartphone

Point your Android camera at this code to download the APK directly to your phone.
